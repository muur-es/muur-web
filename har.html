House in the old flour mill

New build

A house on the last empty plot beside the old flour mill, closed to its neighbours and open to the garden and the hills.

The house is designed for a plot still to be developed on the edge of the village, beside the old flour mill. It is the only unbuilt plot in its surroundings. At the back it borders a group of terraced single-family houses; along its whole front it looks out to the hills.

The house forms a closed courtyard: a garden with a pool, divided into two areas by a stone wall towards the back. The perimeter wall separates the house from the terraced houses, keeps out their views and gives the family a quiet, sheltered space.

On the ground floor the house closes to the street and opens to the garden: the living room and kitchen face the courtyard. Only one window, in the dining room, looks out to the hills. It can be seen from the entrance and is the first to welcome those who arrive.

On the first floor the roles are reversed. The facade that opens to the garden below is closed above, to avoid views from the neighbouring houses. Instead, all the bedrooms and the study open to the hills. Thanks to the shape of the roof, the study reaches almost double height, with a very tall bookcase full of books.

The house is built mainly from two materials: dry stone and white render. Inside, traditional local ceramics are added.

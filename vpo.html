Social housing in Mallorca

Multi-family housing

Our proposal for IBAVI's competition for social housing in Palma de Mallorca.

This project was our entry to a competition for social housing in Palma, organised by IBAVI. It did not win a prize.

The plot was very long and narrow. Its shape, local, regional and national regulations, including the Spanish building code, and the high housing density required by the brief left little room to arrange the apartments.

Orientation also needed great care, above all to the west. A bedroom facing west overheats in summer with the afternoon sun, and then it is impossible to sleep. So we placed the bedrooms facing the street, to the east. The flats have cross ventilation and are reached by an interior gallery open to a garden, which encourages neighbours to meet and a somewhat more shared way of life.

The most distinctive element is the facade: a skin of perforated sheet metal, painted white, that lets light through and lights the flats, while from inside you still sense the outdoors. Rhombus patterns are engraved into the sheet, taken from the textiles once made in the factory opposite.

Inside, each flat has only a short stretch of facade. To make cross ventilation work, we set one of the bedrooms back from the main facade and created a large terrace, both indoor and outdoor thanks to Mallorca's climate. The set-back bedroom opens onto this terrace, and instead of an interior room without light or ventilation it becomes a good space to live in.

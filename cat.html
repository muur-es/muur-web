House for Marije

New build

A single-storey house that turns its back on the approach and opens completely to the surrounding hills.

From the approach the facade is almost a wall: only the front door shows. It is once you step inside that the house opens up and the landscape reaches every room. No room is without a view of the hills.

The plan follows a very simple structure. That regularity makes construction more straightforward and helps keep costs down, and it also gives the house a clear layout that is easy to read from inside.

The living area brings together sitting room, dining room and kitchen in a single space. A curtain can divide it when needed, without losing the sense of space the rest of the time. At each end of the house, a covered porch extends this space outdoors.

There are three bedrooms. The children's two simple rooms share a bathroom. The main bedroom, with its own bathroom, sits at the opposite end of the house, so parents and children each have their own part of it.
